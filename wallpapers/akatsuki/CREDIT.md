Wallpaper: original cloud pattern generated for this repo (themes/.config/themes/themes/akatsuki.conf
palette), inspired by the red-cloud-on-black motif associated with the Akatsuki organization in
Naruto (Masashi Kishimoto / Studio Pierrot). No copyrighted artwork, screenshots, or fan art were
used or copied — the shapes are procedurally generated (see the generation notes in this repo's
history) and the colours are original hex values, not extracted from any copyrighted source.
